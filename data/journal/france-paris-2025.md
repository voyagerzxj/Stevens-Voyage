五月的巴黎是整年里最温柔的时候。栗树开花，塞纳河沿岸的旧书摊重新支起，咖啡馆把桌椅搬到人行道上，整座城市像是刚刚睡醒，慢慢舒展开来。我这次来不是为了打卡——埃菲尔铁塔上次已经爬过了——而是想在某几个街区里漫无目的地走，看看一座城市在没有游客滤镜时是什么样子。

![玛黑区的清晨，石板街道上几乎没有人](https://picsum.photos/seed/paris-marais/1200/700)

住在玛黑区（Le Marais）的小公寓里，楼下就是一家面包店，每天早晨六点准时飘来黄油可颂的香气。我的日程只有一条原则：不赶时间。卢浮宫我只看了两个展厅，在《米洛的维纳斯》前站了很久，然后就去博物馆的咖啡厅喝咖啡、看人。蓬皮杜中心的露台可以俯瞰整个巴黎老城，比埃菲尔铁塔顶上的视角更有层次感，而且排队的人少得多。

::: tip
博物馆通票（Museum Pass）2/4/6天版本非常划算，可以跳过大多数景点的售票队伍直接进入，卢浮宫和凡尔赛宫都适用。五月是旺季，建议提前在官网购买。
:::

<!--en-->
May is Paris at its gentlest. Chestnut trees flower, the bouquiniste stalls along the Seine reopen, cafés push tables onto the pavement, and the city seems to stretch and yawn after winter. I wasn't here to tick boxes — I'd done the Eiffel Tower last time — but to wander aimlessly through a few neighbourhoods and see what the city looks like without the tourist filter.

![Le Marais at 8 a.m. — the cobblestones nearly empty](https://picsum.photos/seed/paris-marais/1200/700)

I stayed in a small flat in Le Marais; a boulangerie downstairs filled the stairwell with the smell of butter croissants every morning at six. My only rule: no rushing. I spent just two galleries at the Louvre, stood a long time in front of the Venus de Milo, then retreated to the museum café to drink coffee and watch people. The Pompidou Centre's rooftop terrace offers a layered panorama of old Paris that feels more honest than the Eiffel Tower's summit — and the queue is a fraction of the length.
