在欧洲旅行者的地图上，阿尔巴尼亚常常是一片空白。但正是这种「未被发现」的气质，让这里的亚得里亚海海岸、古老城堡和热情好客的当地人，带给我意想不到的惊喜。从地拉那出发，开车两小时便抵达了南部的希马里海岸——几乎没有团队游客，海水透明到可以直接看见海底的鹅卵石。

![希马里海岸：蓝绿色的亚得里亚海，人烟稀少](https://picsum.photos/seed/himara-coast/1200/700)

贝拉特古城是这次旅行最让我难忘的地方。这座被称为「千窗之城」的山城，白色奥斯曼时期的老房子密密麻麻地叠在山坡上，每栋房子都有大量朝向河谷的窗户，在阳光下闪闪发光。沿着石板路走进老城，时间仿佛停滞了——卖无花果的老人、在门口织毛衣的妇女、懒洋洋穿过小巷的猫，一切都慢下来了。

::: tip
实用信息：消费水平极低，餐厅正餐含饮料折合人民币约30—50元。推荐尝试当地的 byrek（层层叠叠的馅饼）和 tavë kosi（烤羊肉配酸奶）。出行建议租车，公共交通班次少且不准时。出发前确认签证要求。
:::

如果你在规划巴尔干之旅，不要跳过阿尔巴尼亚。它不像意大利那样精致，不像希腊那样闻名，但它有一种未经修饰的真实感，以及让人意外的安全感和温暖。当地人对外国游客的好奇和热情，是我在西欧旅行时很少感受到的。

<!--en-->
On most travelers' maps of Europe, Albania is a blank spot. But it's precisely this 'undiscovered' quality that makes its Adriatic coastline, ancient fortresses, and extraordinarily warm people such a revelation. Two hours south of Tirana lies the Himara coast — almost no package tourists, the water so clear you can see the pebbles on the bottom.

![Himara Coast: turquoise Adriatic, almost no crowds](https://picsum.photos/seed/himara-coast/1200/700)

Berat old town was the most unforgettable place of the entire trip. Called the 'City of a Thousand Windows', its white Ottoman houses cascade down the hillside, each facade studded with large windows that catch the sun. Walking the cobblestone lanes, time seems to stop — an old man selling figs, a woman knitting in a doorway, a cat drifting across an alley. Everything slows down.

::: tip
Practicalities: Prices are very low — a full restaurant meal with drinks costs roughly €4–7. Try local byrek (flaky layered pastry) and tavë kosi (baked lamb with yogurt). Renting a car is strongly recommended; public transport is infrequent and unreliable. Check visa requirements before you go.
:::

If you're planning a Balkan trip, don't skip Albania. It lacks Italy's polish and Greece's fame, but it has a rawness and authenticity — and a surprising warmth and safety — that makes you want to stay longer. The local curiosity and friendliness toward foreign visitors is something I rarely feel in Western Europe.
