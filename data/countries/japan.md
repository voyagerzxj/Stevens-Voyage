## geography
日本是位于东亚的岛国，由本州、四国、九州、北海道四大岛及众多小岛组成。国土多山，平原面积狭小。地处环太平洋火山带，地震和火山活动频繁。富士山是日本最高峰，高3776米。

## history
日本有着超过两千年的有记载历史。经历绳文、弥生文化，奈良、平安时代的贵族文化，镰仓、室町、江户时代的武士文化，明治维新后迅速现代化。二战后在美国帮助下重建，成为世界经济强国。

## economy
日本是世界第三大经济体，以汽车、电子、精密机械等制造业闻名全球。丰田、索尼、本田等跨国企业享誉世界。近年来日本积极推动数字化转型和旅游业发展。

## population
日本人口约1.25亿，近年来持续减少，面临严重的老龄化和低生育率问题。日本社会高度同质化，外国移民比例较低。东京都市圈是世界上人口最多的大都市区之一，约3800万人。

## culture
日本文化独具魅力，融合传统与现代。茶道、花道、歌舞伎、能剧等传统艺术与动漫、游戏、时尚等现代流行文化并存。日本饮食以寿司、拉面、天妇罗等闻名，注重食材新鲜与精致摆盘。樱花季是日本最具代表性的文化符号之一。

## geography
Japan is an island nation in East Asia comprising four main islands — Honshu, Shikoku, Kyushu, Hokkaido — and thousands of smaller islands. Mountainous terrain covers most of the land. Located on the Pacific Ring of Fire, earthquakes and volcanic activity are frequent. Mount Fuji, at 3,776 m, is the highest peak.

## history
Japan has over 2,000 years of recorded history, from Jomon and Yayoi cultures through aristocratic Nara and Heian periods, to samurai culture in Kamakura, Muromachi and Edo periods. The Meiji Restoration launched rapid modernization. After World War II, Japan rebuilt with American assistance to become a global economic powerhouse.

## economy
Japan is the world's third-largest economy, renowned for automobile, electronics and precision machinery manufacturing. Companies like Toyota, Sony and Honda are global icons. In recent years Japan has actively promoted digital transformation and tourism.

## population
Japan's population of ~125 million is declining due to severe aging and low birth rates. Japanese society is highly homogeneous with low immigration. The Greater Tokyo Area, with ~38 million people, is one of the world's most populous metropolitan areas.

## culture
Japanese culture uniquely blends tradition and modernity. Traditional arts like tea ceremony, ikebana, kabuki and noh coexist with modern anime, gaming and fashion. Japanese cuisine — sushi, ramen, tempura — emphasizes fresh ingredients and meticulous presentation. Cherry blossom season is one of Japan's most iconic cultural symbols.
