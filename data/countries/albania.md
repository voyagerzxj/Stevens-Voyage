## geography
阿尔巴尼亚位于东南欧巴尔干半岛西部，西临亚得里亚海和爱奥尼亚海，北接黑山和科索沃，东接北马其顿，南邻希腊。国土面积约28,748平方公里，地形以山地为主，70%以上为山地和丘陵。阿尔卑斯山南端延伸至北部，形成壮观的阿尔巴尼亚阿尔卑斯山（又称阿克塞迪内山脉）。德林河、塞曼河等是主要河流。海岸线长约476公里，拥有清澈碧蓝的亚得里亚海与爱奥尼亚海海滩。

## history
阿尔巴尼亚是古伊利里亚人的故乡，公元前7世纪希腊人在此建立殖民地。罗马帝国统治后，历经拜占庭帝国、中世纪阿尔巴尼亚公国，1468年后被奥斯曼帝国征服并统治长达约450年。民族英雄斯坎德培（1405-1468）组织了对奥斯曼帝国的长期抵抗，被视为阿尔巴尼亚的国家英雄。1912年11月28日，阿尔巴尼亚宣布独立，成为巴尔干地区最后独立的国家之一。二战后建立共产主义政权，在恩维尔·霍查领导下实行极端封闭的孤立主义政策长达40余年，1990年代初才开放并转型为民主国家。目前正积极推进欧盟入盟进程。

## economy
阿尔巴尼亚经济近年来持续稳定增长，旅游业是最重要的增长引擎，每年吸引游客数量屡创新高。水电资源丰富，电力出口是重要收入来源。农业（蔬菜、水果、橄榄油）和纺织制造业也是支柱产业。大量阿尔巴尼亚人在意大利、希腊、德国等欧洲国家工作，侨汇收入对GDP贡献显著。石油和天然气资源正在开发中。地拉那作为区域性商业中心，近年来外资流入明显增加。

## population
阿尔巴尼亚人口约280万，是欧洲人口较少的国家之一。由于大量移民，实际境内人口有所减少。阿尔巴尼亚族占总人口约95%，其余为希腊族、马其顿族等少数民族。历史上伊斯兰教、东正教和天主教并存，阿尔巴尼亚以宗教包容著称。首都地拉那约有50-80万人，是国内唯一的大城市。

## culture
阿尔巴尼亚文化独具一格，以"贝萨"（Besa，意为"信守承诺"）这一荣誉准则最为著名，二战期间许多阿尔巴尼亚家庭因此庇护了数百名犹太难民。传统的"等声复调"（Iso-polyphony）无伴奏合唱已被联合国教科文组织列为人类非物质文化遗产。民间舞蹈、手工地毯（基利姆）和精美的银器工艺历史悠久。阿尔巴尼亚美食深受地中海和奥斯曼饮食传统影响，烤肉、奶酪派（Byrek）、发酵乳（Kos）和各类肉类菜肴是代表性食品。

## geography
Albania is in the western Balkans of southeastern Europe, bordering the Adriatic and Ionian Seas to the west, Montenegro and Kosovo to the north, North Macedonia to the east, and Greece to the south. Covering 28,748 km², the terrain is predominantly mountainous — over 70% hills and mountains. The southern Albanian Alps (Accursed Mountains) dominate the north. Major rivers include the Drin and Seman. The 476 km coastline features the crystal-clear waters of the Adriatic and Ionian Seas.

## history
Albania is the homeland of the ancient Illyrians; Greeks established colonies here in the 7th century BCE. After Roman rule, it passed through the Byzantine Empire and medieval Albanian principalities, before being conquered by the Ottoman Empire in 1468 — a rule lasting ~450 years. National hero Skanderbeg (1405–1468) led prolonged resistance against the Ottomans and remains Albania's greatest national symbol. Independence was declared on 28 November 1912, one of the last Balkan nations to do so. After WWII, a communist regime under Enver Hoxha enforced extreme isolation for over 40 years. Albania opened up in the early 1990s and is now actively pursuing EU membership.

## economy
Albania's economy has grown steadily in recent years, with tourism as the most important growth engine — visitor numbers reach new highs annually. Rich hydropower resources make electricity export a key revenue source. Agriculture (vegetables, fruit, olive oil) and textile manufacturing are also pillars. A large Albanian diaspora in Italy, Greece and Germany sends significant remittances home. Oil and gas resources are being developed. Tirana, as a regional commercial center, has attracted increasing foreign investment.

## population
Albania has ~2.8 million people and is one of Europe's smaller nations. Significant emigration has reduced the resident population. Albanians make up ~95% of the population; the remainder are Greeks, Macedonians and others. Historically Islam, Eastern Orthodoxy and Catholicism have coexisted — Albania is renowned for remarkable religious tolerance. Tirana, with 500,000–800,000 people, is the only large city.

## culture
Albanian culture is unique and defined by Besa — a code of honor meaning 'keeping the promise,' which led many Albanian families to shelter hundreds of Jewish refugees during WWII. Traditional polyphonic singing (Iso-polyphony), performed without instruments, is listed as UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage. Folk dancing, handwoven kilim rugs and fine silverware have long traditions. Albanian cuisine blends Mediterranean and Ottoman influences: grilled meats, byrek (savory pastry), kos (fermented yogurt) and hearty meat dishes are staples.
