## geography
巴西是南美洲最大的国家，占南美洲面积近一半。亚马逊热带雨林覆盖大部分西北部地区，是地球上最大的热带雨林。亚马逊河是世界流量最大的河流。南部高原地区气候温和，适宜农业发展。

## history
巴西于1500年被葡萄牙航海家卡布拉尔发现，此后成为葡萄牙殖民地长达三个世纪，引入了大量非洲奴隶从事甘蔗种植。1822年独立建立帝国，1889年成为共和国。20世纪经历了军事独裁时期，1985年恢复民主。

## economy
巴西是南美洲最大经济体，也是世界第九大经济体。农业极为发达，是世界最大的大豆、橙汁、咖啡和蔗糖生产国之一。石油、矿产、制造业和服务业也是重要支柱。里约热内卢和圣保罗是主要经济中心。

## population
巴西人口约2.15亿，是种族最多元的国家之一，融合了原住民、欧洲移民（尤其是葡萄牙裔）、非洲裔和亚裔（尤其是日裔）。天主教是主要宗教，种族融合产生了独特的巴西文化。

## culture
巴西文化充满热情与活力，桑巴舞、巴萨诺瓦音乐和里约嘉年华举世闻名。足球是巴西的国民宗教，巴西队曾五次夺得世界杯冠军。巴西烤肉（Churrasco）和热带水果是饮食特色，亚马逊原住民文化丰富多彩。

## geography
Brazil is South America's largest country, occupying nearly half the continent. The Amazon rainforest covers most of the northwest — Earth's largest tropical forest. The Amazon River has the world's greatest water discharge. The southern plateau has a mild climate suitable for agriculture.

## history
Brazil was 'discovered' by Portuguese navigator Cabral in 1500 and became a colony for three centuries, importing millions of African slaves for sugar plantations. Independence came in 1822 as an empire; a republic was declared in 1889. The 20th century included military dictatorship; democracy was restored in 1985.

## economy
Brazil is South America's largest economy and the world's ninth-largest. Agriculture is highly developed — it is among the world's largest producers of soybeans, orange juice, coffee and sugar. Oil, minerals, manufacturing and services are also key. Rio de Janeiro and São Paulo are the main economic centers.

## population
Brazil's ~215 million people make it one of the world's most racially diverse nations, blending Indigenous, European (especially Portuguese), African and Asian (especially Japanese) heritage. Catholicism is dominant; racial mixing has created a unique Brazilian culture.

## culture
Brazilian culture is vibrant and passionate. Samba, Bossa Nova and Rio Carnival are world-famous. Football is a national religion — Brazil has won the World Cup five times. Churrasco (BBQ) and tropical fruits define the cuisine. Amazon Indigenous cultures are richly diverse.
