## geography
埃及位于非洲东北部，大部分国土为撒哈拉沙漠所覆盖。尼罗河自南向北贯穿全境，在北部形成肥沃的尼罗河三角洲。苏伊士运河连接地中海和红海，具有重要的战略意义。

## history
古埃及文明是世界四大文明古国之一，可追溯至公元前3100年。法老王朝建造了金字塔、狮身人面像等不朽建筑。经历希腊、罗马、阿拉伯、奥斯曼帝国统治后，1952年纳赛尔领导革命建立共和国。

## economy
埃及经济以旅游业、苏伊士运河收入、石油天然气、侨汇和农业为主。尼罗河沿岸是重要农业区，棉花是传统出口产品。近年来大力发展工业和新城建设，建设新行政首都。

## population
埃及人口约1.05亿，是非洲人口最多的国家之一。绝大多数埃及人是阿拉伯裔，伊斯兰教（逊尼派）是国教，科普特基督徒约占10%。开罗是非洲最大的城市，人口超过2000万。

## culture
埃及文化是阿拉伯文化的重要组成部分，同时保留着法老时代的独特遗产。开罗是阿拉伯世界的文化中心，埃及电影和音乐在整个阿拉伯世界具有重要影响力。象形文字、木乃伊和金字塔是人类共同的文化瑰宝。

## geography
Egypt is in northeastern Africa; most of its territory is covered by the Sahara Desert. The Nile flows south to north through the country, forming a fertile delta in the north. The Suez Canal connects the Mediterranean and Red Sea and is of immense strategic importance.

## history
Ancient Egyptian civilization is one of the world's four great ancient civilizations, dating to 3100 BCE. Pharaonic dynasties built pyramids and the Sphinx. After Greek, Roman, Arab and Ottoman rule, Nasser's revolution established a republic in 1952.

## economy
Egypt's economy depends on tourism, Suez Canal revenues, oil and gas, remittances and agriculture. The Nile Valley is a key agricultural zone; cotton is a traditional export. Egypt is developing industry and new cities, including a new administrative capital.

## population
Egypt has ~105 million people, one of Africa's most populous nations. Most Egyptians are Arab; Sunni Islam is the state religion, with Coptic Christians comprising ~10%. Cairo is Africa's largest city with over 20 million people.

## culture
Egyptian culture is a key part of Arab culture while retaining its unique Pharaonic heritage. Cairo is the cultural capital of the Arab world; Egyptian cinema and music are hugely influential across the Arab world. Hieroglyphics, mummies and pyramids are treasures of human civilization.
