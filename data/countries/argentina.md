## geography
阿根廷是南美洲第二大国，地形从北部热带查科平原，经过中部潘帕斯草原，到南部巴塔哥尼亚高原和安第斯山脉，再到南端的火地岛。伊瓜苏瀑布是世界最宽的瀑布之一。

## history
阿根廷原住民有数千年历史，1516年西班牙人抵达，1816年宣布独立。19世纪大量欧洲移民涌入，尤以意大利和西班牙裔为主。20世纪经历多次军事政变和庇隆主义政治，1983年恢复民主制度。

## economy
阿根廷是南美洲第三大经济体，农业（大豆、牛肉、小麦）极为发达。布宜诺斯艾利斯是重要的金融中心。阿根廷拥有丰富的锂矿资源，有望成为重要的锂生产国。近年来通货膨胀严峻，经济面临挑战。

## population
阿根廷人口约4600万，绝大多数是欧洲裔（尤以意大利和西班牙后裔为主），是拉丁美洲白人比例最高的国家之一。天主教是主要宗教，布宜诺斯艾利斯素有'南美巴黎'之称。

## culture
探戈舞起源于布宜诺斯艾利斯，已成为阿根廷最具代表性的文化符号，被列入联合国非物质文化遗产。阿根廷是足球强国，梅西是国家英雄。马黛茶是日常饮品，烤牛肉（Asado）是最重要的饮食传统。

## geography
Argentina is South America's second-largest country, with terrain ranging from northern tropical Chaco plains through the Pampas grasslands, to Patagonia and the Andes, to Tierra del Fuego in the far south. Iguazu Falls is one of the world's widest waterfalls.

## history
Argentina's Indigenous peoples have millennia of history. Spanish arrived in 1516; independence was declared in 1816. The 19th century brought massive European immigration, mainly Italian and Spanish. The 20th century saw multiple military coups and Peronism; democracy was restored in 1983.

## economy
Argentina is South America's third-largest economy with highly developed agriculture (soybeans, beef, wheat). Buenos Aires is a major financial center. Argentina has vast lithium reserves and is set to become a key lithium producer. Recent years have seen severe inflation and economic challenges.

## population
Argentina has ~46 million people, predominantly of European descent (especially Italian and Spanish), giving it one of the highest white proportions in Latin America. Catholicism is dominant. Buenos Aires is nicknamed the 'Paris of South America.'

## culture
Tango originated in Buenos Aires and is Argentina's most iconic cultural symbol, listed as UNESCO intangible heritage. Argentina is a football powerhouse; Messi is a national hero. Mate tea is the daily drink; Asado (barbecue) is the most important culinary tradition.
