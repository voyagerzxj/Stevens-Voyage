## geography
新西兰由北岛和南岛两大主岛及众多小岛组成，位于太平洋西南部。地形以山地为主，南岛的南阿尔卑斯山脉有多座海拔超过3000米的高峰。峡湾国家公园以其壮观的冰川峡湾著称，米尔福德峡湾是最著名的景观。

## history
毛利人约在13世纪从东波利尼西亚航行至新西兰定居。1642年荷兰探险家塔斯曼最早抵达，1769年库克船长详细考察。1840年签订的《怀唐伊条约》确立了英国主权与毛利人权利的关系，是新西兰建国的基础文件。

## economy
新西兰经济以农牧业（乳制品、羊肉、牛肉、羊毛、苹果）、旅游业和教育业为主。乳制品是最重要的出口产品，恒天然是全球最大的乳制品出口商之一。新西兰因清洁绿色的形象在全球享有盛誉。

## population
新西兰人口约500万，主要由欧裔新西兰人（帕克哈）和毛利人组成，近年来亚裔移民比例增加。毛利文化在新西兰社会中具有重要地位，毛利语是官方语言之一。新西兰是世界上最早赋予女性选举权的国家（1893年）。

## culture
新西兰文化以毛利文化和欧洲文化的融合为特色。毛利人的哈卡战舞（由全黑橄榄球队演绎）享誉全球。新西兰以《指环王》《霍比特人》拍摄地闻名，被称为'中土世界'。橄榄球（全黑队）是国民运动，自然探险和户外运动也极为流行。

## geography
New Zealand consists of the North Island, South Island and many smaller islands in the southwestern Pacific. Terrain is predominantly mountainous; the Southern Alps in the South Island have several peaks over 3,000 m. Fiordland National Park is famous for spectacular glacial fiords, with Milford Sound the most celebrated.

## history
Māori people arrived from eastern Polynesia around the 13th century. Dutch explorer Tasman reached New Zealand in 1642; Captain Cook surveyed it in 1769. The 1840 Treaty of Waitangi established the relationship between British sovereignty and Māori rights and is New Zealand's founding document.

## economy
New Zealand's economy is led by agriculture (dairy, lamb, beef, wool, apples), tourism and education. Dairy is the most important export; Fonterra is one of the world's largest dairy exporters. New Zealand enjoys a global reputation for being clean and green.

## population
New Zealand has ~5 million people, mainly Pākehā (European New Zealanders) and Māori, with a growing proportion of Asian immigrants. Māori culture is central to New Zealand identity; Māori is an official language. New Zealand was the first country to grant women the right to vote (1893).

## culture
New Zealand culture blends Māori and European heritage. The Māori haka war dance (performed by the All Blacks rugby team) is world-famous. New Zealand is known as 'Middle-earth' for The Lord of the Rings and The Hobbit filming locations. Rugby (the All Blacks) is the national sport; adventure tourism and outdoor activities are hugely popular.
